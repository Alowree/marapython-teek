Looking at the options you’ve provided, the most important setting for FBA fulfillment is under the **Cart & Checkout** section:

### The Key Setting: `Cart contents count`

Currently, yours is set to: **`Bundled products only`**

This is actually the **correct** setting for your goal. It tells WooCommerce to treat the "children" (the individual items) as the primary contents of the cart for shipping and fulfillment purposes.

However, because your FBA plugin is still trying to send the "Bundle Header" (resulting in that `4736-item-0-` prefix), you need to adjust two more things that aren't in this global settings list, but are likely on the **individual Product Edit page** for each bundle.

---

## 1. Action: The "Virtual" Parent Strategy

To prevent the FBA plugin from seeing the Bundle Header as a physical item:

1. Open the **Sound Spot II — Black Bundle** (or any Smart Bundle) in the WordPress editor.
2. In the **Product Data** box (where you chose "Smart Bundle"):
   - **[Check] the "Virtual" box.**
3. Scroll down to the **Smart Bundle** tab (where you added the products):
   - Ensure the **individual products** (like the Simple #4157 / SFQ-18BLK) are **NOT** marked as virtual.

**Why this works:** The FBA plugin ignores "Virtual" products. By making the **Parent** virtual but the **Children** physical, WPClever passes the physical children to the FBA plugin, while the FBA plugin ignores the "Smart Bundle" header entirely.

---

## 2. Action: Remove the Parent SKU

For the **Sound Spot II — Black Bundle**:

- **Remove `SFQ-18BLKx2` from the SKU field.**
- If the Parent has no SKU and is marked "Virtual," the FBA plugin will have no "SellerSKU" to grab for the header, forcing it to only look at the items WPClever has "exploded" into the cart (the 2 units of `SFQ-18BLK`).

---

## 3. Dealing with "SFQ-16+FC14C-BK"

You mentioned you changed the SKU here to `SFQ16+SFQ14C-BLK` to match Amazon.

- **Crucial Logic Check:** If `SFQ16+SFQ14C-BLK` is a **single SKU** on Amazon that contains both items in one box, keep it as it is and **DO NOT** make the parent virtual.
- **If they are separate boxes:** Follow the "Virtual Parent" strategy above and remove the SKU.

---

## Summary of the "Missing" Fields

WPClever's global settings (the ones you pasted) control the _display_. The _fulfillment_ logic is usually handled per-product. Check the **"Bundles"** tab inside the specific product edit page for these two specific toggles:

1. **Shipping:** Look for "Shipment" or "Shipping" and see if it is set to **"Bundled products"** or **"Bundle as a whole."** You want "Bundled products."
2. **Item Data:** Ensure it is set to pass the individual SKUs to the order.

### Your Debugging Checklist for the Next Order

- [ ] **Parent SKU:** Deleted (unless it's a pre-packed Amazon SKU).
- [ ] **Parent Status:** Virtual (to hide it from FBA plugin).
- [ ] **Child SKUs:** Must match Amazon exactly (e.g., `SFQ-18BLK`).
